Hanjang Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-28 · 한국어

Hanjang collects no personal data. There is no sign-up or login, and everything you shoot and print is processed and stored only on this device. Your photos never leave your device.

1 Personal data we collect

Hanjang collects no personal data. There is no sign-up or login, and no name, email, phone number, location or any other data is sent to a server.

2 How photos are handled

Photos you shoot or pick from your library, and the prints you make, are processed and stored only on this device. Hanjang runs no server and never sends photos over the network.

3 Permissions

PermissionPurpose
CameraTo shoot a photo and print it as a card.
Add to PhotosTo save a print to your photo library.
Read PhotosTo pick a photo from your library and print it.

Permissions are requested when you first use the feature; if you decline, everything else keeps working.

4 Sharing

"Hand over" uses the operating system's share sheet as is. You choose where it goes; Hanjang keeps no record of what you shared.

5 Analytics and ads

Hanjang uses no analytics tools and no advertising SDKs.

6 Retention and deletion

Prints stay in the app's film box until you throw them away, or until you delete the app.

7 Children

Since Hanjang collects no personal data, the same policy applies regardless of age.

8 Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated with an app update and the effective date will be shown.
